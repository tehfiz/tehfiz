Getting started

What is Tehfīz, and who is it for?

Tehfīz is a free web app for Quran teachers. You use it while you listen to a student recite, online or in person: you tap the word they stumbled on, record how much help you gave, and send them the lesson when you finish. It suits teachers of hifz, tajweed and revision, with one student or many.

What do I need?

A phone, tablet or computer with a browser. There is nothing to download. Teachers sign in with a Google account. Students never need an account: they use a code you give them and a PIN they choose.

How do I get a teacher account?
  1. Open the teacher app.
  2. Tap Students in the top bar and sign in with Google.
  3. A new account waits for approval, which we do by hand. Marking lessons and saving them on your device works while you wait. Only sending to students is held back.
How do I add a student?

Type the student's name in the session panel and choose create code, or use New student in the Students panel. Each student gets a code like HUDHUD-S6PX. The code is their identity on Tehfīz. Nothing else needs setting up.

How does my student log in?
  1. Give them the code, or send it to a parent.
  2. They open the student viewer and tap Log in.
  3. They enter the code and choose This is my first time.
  4. They pick a 4-number PIN and type it twice.

The PIN is theirs. You never see it.

Is there a tour inside the app?

Yes. Open Menu and choose Take the tour. Press ? at any time for the built-in help and the keyboard shortcuts. There is also a walkthrough video on YouTube.

Marking a lesson

How do I mark a mistake?
  1. Tap the Arabic word on the mushaf.
  2. Pick the kind of mistake.
  3. Pick how much help you gave.
  4. Add a note if it is useful, then press Add or Enter.

For tajweed mistakes you can also name the exact rule.

What do the four levels of help mean?
LevelNameWhat happened
0NoneNo help was given. The student corrected themselves or moved past it.
1RepeatYou asked the student to stop and redo it, without giving any words.
2HintYou gave the next word or a short prompt.
3TaughtYou recited a verse, or a large part of one, for the student to follow.
How do I mark a skipped phrase or a whole verse?
  • Computer: Shift-click the first word, then click the last.
  • Phone or tablet: long-press the first word until it pulses, then tap the last.
  • Whole verse: tap the circle at the end of the verse.

The first and last word must be in the same verse.

How do I change or remove a mistake?

Every mistake appears in the list beside the mushaf. Tap the pencil to change its type, level or note. Tap ✕ to delete it. Tap the card itself to jump to that word.

What is a segment?

A segment is one passage covered in a lesson. A sitting usually holds several, for example a new lesson and an older revision, and they are recorded separately because they should not be judged by the same standard. One segment is active at a time, and new mistakes are logged against it. Tap another segment to switch.

What is the difference between Recite and Spot test?
  • Recite: the student reads the whole passage. The app fills in the range as you go. These passages earn a strength score.
  • Spot test: you sample a range. Set the range, choose how many spots, and the app picks evenly spaced starting points. The measure is the pass rate.
How do I set a range?

Tap a range chip to open the picker and choose Juz, Page, Surah or Ayah. You can type a number, a span such as 2-3, or a surah name. Or choose Tap words instead and tap the first and last word on the mushaf.

I prefer sabaq, sabqi and manzil. Can I use those words?

Yes. The labels for new, recent and old can be switched, and your past sessions keep their meaning.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. While marking, 1 to 6 pick the mistake type and q w e r pick the level. Press ? in the app for the full list. You can change the keys there too.

What is the strength score?

A single figure from 0 to 100 for how solid a passage was. It combines how often the student slipped with how much help each slip needed, measured per verse recited.

ScoreBand
90 and aboveVery strong
75 and aboveStrong
55 and aboveSteady
35 and aboveShaky
Below 35Needs work

It shows a dash when it cannot be measured fairly: spot tests, and passages under 10 verses. Compare like with like, new with new and old with old.

Finishing and sending

How do I send a lesson to my student?
  1. Tap Finish session at the bottom of the session panel.
  2. Check the student and the time.
  3. Tap Send.

You can also use Send on any saved session in Sessions. Nothing is sent by itself. You decide when a lesson becomes visible.

What if I have no signal?

Sending still works. The lesson is queued and goes out by itself once you are back online. Sessions shows it as Queued until then.

Can I hide or delete a lesson I sent?

Yes. In the Students panel, Open in viewer loads that student's history. There you can Hide a session, which can be reversed, or Delete it, which erases it for good. For a lesson logged by mistake, hide it first.

I sent a lesson to the wrong student, or too soon.

On the screen that says it was sent, choose Send to a different student… or Undo send. Undo takes it back off the student's viewer and map and returns you to Finish session, so you can change it and send it again. For an older lesson, open Sessions, then Sent to students, and use the ⋯ menu on it.

Can I keep lessons as files instead?

Yes. A lesson can be saved as a .qrs file and passed on by hand. Sending is an extra route, not a replacement.

My student forgot their PIN, or their code was seen by someone else.

Tap Manage on their row in the Students panel.

  • Reset PIN: the code stays the same and they pick a new PIN.
  • Change code: issues a new code and clears the PIN.

Both sign the student out on every device and stop their calendar link; the viewer asks them to add their calendar again. Afterwards they log in again choosing This is my first time. Their lessons are not affected. A student who mistypes a PIN five times waits fifteen minutes.

What the student sees

What is in the student viewer?

Every mistake highlighted on the mushaf where it happened, with the level of help and your note. The viewer also holds their progress report, their memorised portions and their next lesson.

What does the progress report show?
  • Condition over time, with separate lines for new, recent and old.
  • What keeps coming back: words missed again in later lessons.
  • What to work on next, ranked.
  • Spot test results over time.

The teacher sees the same report.

Can a parent follow along?

Yes. A parent can open the viewer with the student's code and PIN.

Map, next lesson and calendar

What is the memorisation map?

A page-by-page, juz-by-juz picture of what a student has memorised, marked old, recent and new. It is in the student's profile. Each new lesson is added to the map when you finish the session.

How is the next lesson planned?

Set the student's weekly days and times once, in their time zone and yours. After each lesson the next one fills itself in with what to prepare, and the next old portion is suggested by rotation. You can change any of it. The student sees the next lesson in their viewer.

Where is the calendar?

Open Menu and choose Calendar, or press c. It shows the week for all your students.

Can I see lessons in Google Calendar or my phone's calendar?

Yes. The calendar gives you a private link that puts your lessons in your own calendar app. Students can get a link for theirs. Calendar apps fetch the link on their own schedule, so a change can take some hours to show, and Google Calendar is often the slowest.

How do reminders work?

Tehfīz writes the message for you: a reminder, running late, or cancelled. Copy it, or open it in WhatsApp. Tehfīz does not send messages itself.

How do invoices work?

Lessons are timed and totalled per student, by month or for any dates you choose. Open Menu and choose Invoices to print an invoice or mark it paid.

Names and privacy

Does Tehfīz know my students' names?

No. There is no name field in our database. A student is a code. The name beside it stays on your device.

I changed device and the names are gone.

Names live on the device where you typed them, so a new device or cleared browser data shows codes without names. To avoid this, turn on name sync in the Students panel, which keeps a copy in your own Google Drive, or export the roster and keep it somewhere safe.

What do you keep, and for how long?

The privacy page lists everything we store, why, and how to have it deleted.

Installing and working offline

How do I install Tehfīz like an app?
  • Android or computer: open Menu and choose Install the app.
  • iPhone or iPad: open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

The teacher app and the student viewer install separately.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once it has been opened online at least once on that device. You can mark a lesson with no signal, and sending waits until you are back online.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. Open Settings and look under Display to pick a colour theme: Graphite, Lapis, Mulberry or Gold. You can also choose light, dark, or follow your device. This works in both the teacher app and the student viewer.

Organisations and contact

We are a school or academy. Can Tehfīz work for us?

Write to contact@tehfiz.com and tell us how your institution works. We build features for organisations on request, such as an organisation account that adds and removes teachers, assigns students to several teachers, and sees the organisation's data together.

Is it really free?

Yes, for every teacher and every student, with no adverts. If you would like to help with the running costs, you can support Tehfīz on Patreon.

I have room for more students. Can you send families my way?

Yes. In the teacher app, open your teaching profile from Settings, fill it in, and tick Recommend students to me when available.

How do I hear about new features?

Use the Get updates box on the homepage. We send a confirmation email first, and every update has an unsubscribe link. Tehfīz is also on Instagram.

Something is not working. Who do I tell?

Email contact@tehfiz.com. It helps to say which device and browser you use, and what you were doing when it happened.

Still stuck?

Email contact@tehfiz.com and we will help.
