Privacy & Terms

Tehfīz is used by children. This page explains what that means for the information we hold — and, more importantly, what we have deliberately arranged not to hold.

The short version

We do not know your students' names. There is no name field in our database. A student is identified by a code we generate, like HUDHUD-S6PX. The name attached to that code is stored in the teacher's own browser and is never sent to us.

This is a deliberate design decision, not an oversight, and it shapes everything below. Because we never receive a child's name, we cannot disclose it, lose it in a breach, or be compelled to hand it over. We accepted a real cost for this: a teacher who clears their browser data loses their name list and has to rebuild it from their exported backup.

We also do not run advertising, do not sell or share data with anyone, and do not use analytics or tracking of any kind. There are no third-party scripts on the apps beyond Google's sign-in for teachers and the Quran text and fonts described below.

What we store about teachers

Teachers sign in with Google. We never see or store a password.

We request only your email address and basic profile from Google. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else, and we could not read them if we wanted to.

What we store about students

Students never create an account and never give us an email address. They are given a code by their teacher and choose a 4-digit PIN.

WhatNotes
A generated codee.g. HUDHUD-S6PX. Not derived from anything about the student
The PIN, hashedStored so it cannot be reversed, and additionally protected by a key held outside the database. We cannot read a student's PIN, and neither could anyone who obtained a copy of the database alone
Recitation sessionsWhich verses were recited, which words were mistaken, the type of mistake, how much help the teacher gave, and any notes the teacher wrote
Devices signed inStored as a one-way fingerprint so a teacher can sign a lost device out. The actual sign-in token is never stored

What is deliberately absent: no name, no age, no school, no email address, no phone number, no photograph, no location, and no IP address logging by us.

A word about teacher notes

Teachers: the notes you attach to a mistake are free text, and they are stored on our servers along with the session. Please do not write a student's name, their school, or anything else that identifies them in a note. Everything else in the design keeps children unidentifiable; a name typed into a note undoes that.

We cannot detect or prevent this, which is why we ask. Notes are intended for teaching observations — "watch the madd", "rushed the ending" — not for identification.

How long we keep things

  • Sessions are kept until the teacher deletes them, or until the student's code is deleted.
  • Inactive teacher accounts may be deleted after 24 months without a sign-in, along with the codes and sessions belonging to them. We will email you before that happens.
  • Suspended accounts keep their records, because suspension is meant to be reversible. Suspension is not deletion.

Deleting your data

A teacher can permanently delete any session from within the app. When they do, the recitation record is erased from our storage immediately.

A small marker is kept — an identifier and a timestamp, with no recitation content — so that a student's device learns the session is gone and removes its own copy. Without that marker, a deleted session would keep living on the student's phone, and the deletion would be a lie. The marker contains nothing about what was recited.

To delete an entire account, or a specific student's records, email contact@tehfiz.com. Quote the student's code rather than their name — we have no names to match against, so a request naming a child is one we cannot act on. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.

Parents and guardians may make this request as well as teachers.

Enquiry forms

Separately from the apps, we run two forms: one for people looking for a teacher, and one for teachers looking for students. These are Google Forms, and what you send through them goes to us by email — it is not stored in the database that holds recitation sessions, and it is never published anywhere.

  • We ask that enquiries about a child come from a parent or guardian, and we do not ask for a child's own name or contact details.
  • We use what you send only to make an introduction, and only with your agreement.
  • We keep enquiries for up to 12 months in case a suitable match comes along later, then delete them. Ask us at any time to delete yours sooner.
  • Because Google Forms hosts these, your responses are also processed by Google.

We do not verify anyone's qualifications and we do not vouch for teachers or families. An introduction is not a recommendation. Please make your own checks before arranging lessons.

Who else can see it

Two companies process data on our behalf, and no one else:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the site, the application and the database.
  • Google — verifies teacher sign-ins. Google sees that you signed in to Tehfīz; it does not receive any recitation data.

The apps also load Quran text and fonts from quran.com and the King Fahd Complex, which involves your browser requesting those files. No information about you or your students is sent with those requests.

We do not sell data, do not share it with advertisers, and will not transfer it to anyone else without telling you.

Terms of use

The service is free, and provided as it is

Tehfīz costs nothing to use and comes with no guarantee of availability, accuracy or fitness for any particular purpose. It is a teaching aid, not a system of record. Keep your own copies of anything you would be sorry to lose — both apps can export sessions as files at any time, and we recommend using that.

Teachers are responsible for their own students

If you use Tehfīz with students, you decide what is recorded about them and you are responsible for having the appropriate permission from them or their guardians. We provide the tool; the teaching relationship is yours. You are also responsible for keeping student codes and your own account secure.

Accounts require approval

New teacher accounts are reviewed before they can send sessions to students. We may decline or suspend an account, particularly where we believe a child may be at risk. Suspension keeps your records intact.

What you must not do

  • Record identifying information about a child in a note, as described above.
  • Use the service to store anything unrelated to recitation feedback.
  • Attempt to access another teacher's students or sessions.
  • Attempt to disrupt the service for others.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that affects what we store or who can see it, we will say so on the site before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or concerns about a child's safety: contact@tehfiz.com.

We read everything sent to that address and aim to reply within a few days.
