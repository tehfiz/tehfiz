Help & Guide
Understanding your session feedback

What each highlighted colour means

HesitationYou stopped, paused, or repeated a word before continuing.
Wrong wordYou recited an incorrect word — substituted, transposed, or changed from the text.
SkippedYou skipped a word or phrase entirely without reciting it.
MakhrajIncorrect pronunciation of a letter from its point of articulation (makhraj).
TajweedIncorrect application of a tajweed rule — e.g. madd, ghunna, idgham.
OtherA mistake that didn't fit the standard categories — your teacher added a custom note.

How much help your teacher provided at each mistake

Level 0
No intervention — You made a mistake but your teacher did not step in. You either self-corrected, moved past it, or your teacher chose to observe silently.
Level 1
Repeat — Your teacher asked you to stop and try again without giving any words. "Go back and try again" or "go back to the previous verse".
Level 2
Hint — Your teacher gave you the next word or a short prompt to help you continue, without reciting a full verse.
Level 3
Taught — Your teacher had to recite a full verse or a large portion of it for you to follow along and get back on track.
