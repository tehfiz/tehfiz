What each mistake category means

How much help did the teacher provide?

Level 0
No intervention — No help was given. The student either self-corrected, moved past it, or the teacher chose not to step in. (Leave intervention blank to log Level 0.)
Level 1
Repeat — Teacher asked the student to stop and redo without giving any words. "Go back and try again" or "go back to the previous verse".
Level 2
Hint — Teacher gave the next word or a short prompt to get the student back on track, without reciting a full verse.
Level 3
Taught — Teacher had to recite a full verse or a substantial portion for the student to follow.

A segment is one passage covered in a session, and how it was covered. A sitting can hold several — a new lesson and an old revision are different things and shouldn't be judged by the same standard, so they're recorded separately.

📖 Recite — the student reads it all The range is an output: the app fills it in for you. The start is captured when you begin the timer (or log your first mistake), the end when you save. Both show dashed until you confirm them — tap either to correct it.
Because every verse was actually recited, these earn a strength score and a clean-verse count.
🎲 Spot test — you sample it The range is an input: set it first, choose how many spots, then Populate spots. The app picks evenly-spaced starting points across the range and you ask the student to continue from each.
Only a fraction was recited, so there's no verse rate — the honest measure is the pass rate.
The active segment One segment is active at a time, marked with ● and a gold edge. New mistakes are logged against it, and it's the one that stretches if recitation runs past its end. Tap any other segment to switch — inactive ones collapse to a single line to save room.
Setting a range Tap a range chip for the picker: Juz, Page, Surah or Ayah. Type a number, a span like 2-3, or a surah name — "kahf", "Al-Kahf" and "yaseen" all work. Or choose ⌖ Tap words instead and tap the first and last word in the mushaf.
⚠ Every mistake must belong to a segment. If one can't be placed — outside the active spot segment's range, or inside a spot segment whose spots aren't generated yet — it won't be logged, and you'll be offered the ways to fix it. Deleting a segment deletes its mistakes with it.

Memorization strength is a single 0–100 figure for how solid a passage was. It combines how often the student slipped with how much help each slip needed, measured per verse recited — so a whole juz and half a page can be compared fairly.

How each mistake counts Lvl 0 self-corrected — 0.25 · Lvl 1 repeat — 1 · Lvl 2 hint — 2 · Lvl 3 taught — 3.
Level 0 counts a little rather than nothing on purpose: otherwise a recitation full of self-corrections would score exactly the same as a flawless one.
Reading the bands 90+ Very strong · 75+ Strong · 55+ Steady · 35+ Shaky · below 35 Needs work
⚠ Shown as — when it can't be measured honestly: spot-test segments (which sample rather than recite), ranges estimated from older files, and anything under 10 verses, where a single mistake would swing the score wildly.
New memorization naturally carries more mistakes per verse than long-term revision, so compare a score against the same stage — sabaq with sabaq, manzil with manzil — rather than across them.

Instead of saving a .qrs file and passing it on by hand, you can send a finished session straight to a student. They open their viewer and it is there. Files still work exactly as before — this is an extra route, not a replacement.

1 · Sign in Tap 👥 Students in the top bar and sign in with Google. A new account starts on a waiting for approval screen — everything else in the app keeps working while you wait; only sending is held back.
2 · Add a student Type the student's name in the session panel and choose ＋ create code from the dropdown, or use ＋ New student in the Students panel. Each student gets a code like HUDHUD-S6PX. The code is their identity — there is nothing else to set up.
3 · Give them the code Write it down for them, or send it to a parent. Ask them to open the student viewer, tap Log in, enter the code and choose “This is my first time”. They pick a 4-number PIN and type it twice. That PIN is theirs — you never see it, and they should not share it with anyone.
4 · Send a session Finish the session and tap ☁ Send to student in the footer, or Send on any saved session. Nothing goes automatically — you decide when a session becomes visible, so a half-finished one never reaches anyone.
✓ No signal in the masjid? Sending still works. The session is queued and goes out by itself once you are back online. The button reads Queued until it does.
Checking a student's progress In the Students panel, Open in viewer loads that student's whole history into the mushaf view. There you can Hide a session — reversible, the student stops seeing it — or Delete it, which erases the record for good. For a session logged by mistake, hide it first; you can always change your mind.
If a code goes astray Tap Manage on their row. Reset PIN is for a student who has forgotten theirs — their code stays the same and they pick a new PIN next time they log in. Change code is for a code that has been shared or seen by others; it issues a new one and also clears the PIN, because a code and its PIN are handed over together, so a code worth changing has to be assumed to have taken the PIN with it. Both sign out every device, and after either the student logs in again choosing “This is my first time”. Sessions are never affected. A student who mistypes a PIN five times waits fifteen minutes.
⚠ Student names live on this device only — the server never receives them. This protects your students' privacy: they are children, and no identifiable record of them is kept anywhere but with you. The trade is that clearing your browser data or moving to a new device leaves you with codes and no names. Export the roster from the Students panel and keep it somewhere safe.

Every mistake is anchored to a word. Tap one word for a single-word mistake, or select a run of words for a phrase — both open the same form.

① One word — tap it Tap any Arabic word in the mushaf. Pick a mistake type (what went wrong) and an intervention level (how much help it needed), add a note if useful, then press Enter or + Add.
Keys 1–5 choose the type, Q/W/E/R the level, Escape cancels.
② A phrase — select a run of words For a skipped phrase or a run of words that broke down together, select from the first word to the last. Both must be in the same verse.
Desktop — Shift-click the first word, then click the last.
Mobile — long-press the first word until it pulses and the "Multi-word mode" notice appears, then tap the last.
Whole verse — tap the gold circle at the end of the verse.
Escape, or tapping a word in a different verse, cancels the selection.
Editing and removing Every mistake appears in the list on the right. Tap the pencil to change its type, level or note; tap ✕ to delete it. Tap the card itself to jump to that word in the mushaf.
⚠ A mistake has to fall inside the active segment. If it doesn't, it isn't logged and you'll be shown why, with the ways to fix it — see the Segments section.

To log a mistake spanning more than one word (e.g. a skipped phrase or multi-word makhraj error), select a range of words within the same verse.

📱 Mobile — long press Long-press the first word until the pulse animation and "Multi-word mode" notice appear. Then tap the last word in the same verse to confirm.
🖥 Desktop — Shift + click Hold Shift and click the first word. Words between start and cursor are previewed as you hover. Click the last word to confirm.
🔵 Whole verse — tap the verse number Tap the gold circle at the end of any verse to instantly select the entire verse. On desktop, hovering the circle previews all words highlighted.
⚠ Manual range selection must stay within a single verse. Press Escape or tap a word from a different verse to cancel range mode.
