Mark a hifz lesson on the mushaf itself

Tehfīz is a free web app for Quran teachers. Use it while you listen to a student recite, online or in person: tap the word they stumbled on, record how much help you gave, and send them the lesson when you finish.

Who it is for

Teachers of hifz, tajweed and revision, with one student or many. Students and parents see the feedback in their own viewer.

When to use it

During the lesson, as you listen: on a video call or in the same room. You mark on the mushaf as they recite, with nothing to type.

What you need

A phone, tablet or computer with a browser. Teachers sign in with Google; students use a code and a PIN.

The lesson

Built around how hifz is actually taught

One sitting is rarely one thing: new memorisation, this week's revision, and older portions kept alive. Tehfīz records all three separately, in one session.

01

Set your ranges

Mark each passage and tag it new, recent or old. Prefer sabaq, sabqi and manzil? Switch the labels; your past sessions keep their meaning.

02

Tap the mistake

Tap the word on the mushaf, or drag across a skipped phrase. Choose the kind of slip and how much help you gave. For tajweed, name the exact rule.

03

Finish and send

One screen ends the lesson: send it to the student, update their map and set the next lesson. No signal? It queues and goes when you are back.

The part that matters

How much help did you give?

A student who paused and recovered has not made the same mistake as one who had to be told the verse. Tehfīz records the difference every time.

Why it is worth logging

One lesson tells you little. Thirty tell you everything.

  • Condition over time. A separate line for new, recent and old, because they answer different questions.
  • What keeps coming back. The words missed in three sessions months apart. That is the list worth drilling.
  • Climbing or declining. Each revised passage is marked, so you see what needs attention before it is gone.
  • Spot tests. Random verses from any range, tracked over time.
  • A report you can send. A summary for a parent, a spreadsheet, or a PDF.
Between lessons

Every student, kept in order

Teaching several students means remembering who knows what and when each one is next. Tehfīz now keeps that for you.

A map of what is memorised

Page by page, juz by juz: old, recent and new. Each new lesson is added as you finish, and the next old portion is suggested by rotation.

The next lesson, already planned

Weekly days and times per student, in their time zone and yours. The next lesson fills itself in, with what to prepare, and the student sees it too.

One calendar for all students

The whole week at a glance, and a private link that puts lessons in your own calendar app, and your students' in theirs.

Reminders, ready for WhatsApp

Reminder, running late or cancelled: the message is already written. Copy it or open it in WhatsApp.

Hours and invoices

Lessons are timed and totalled per student, by month or any dates you choose. Print the invoice or mark it paid.

Your own grade

Add a grade to a lesson or to each part of it. Where you give one, it leads the report.

Your students are children

We do not know their names

There is no name field in our database. A student is a code we generate, like HUDHUD-S6PX. The name beside it stays on your device, or in your own Google Drive if you turn on name sync.
