What each mistake category means

How much help did the teacher provide?

Level 0
No intervention — No help was given. The student either self-corrected, moved past it, or the teacher chose not to step in. (Leave intervention blank to log Level 0.)
Level 1
Repeat — Teacher asked the student to stop and redo without giving any words. "Go back and try again" or "go back to the previous verse".
Level 2
Hint — Teacher gave the next word or a short prompt to get the student back on track, without reciting a full verse.
Level 3
Taught — Teacher had to recite a full verse or a substantial portion for the student to follow.

To log a mistake spanning more than one word (e.g. a skipped phrase or multi-word makhraj error), select a range of words within the same verse.

📱 Mobile — long press Long-press the first word until the pulse animation and "Multi-word mode" notice appear. Then tap the last word in the same verse to confirm.
🖥 Desktop — Shift + click Hold Shift and click the first word. Words between start and cursor are previewed as you hover. Click the last word to confirm.
🔵 Whole verse — tap the verse number Tap the gold circle at the end of any verse to instantly select the entire verse. On desktop, hovering the circle previews all words highlighted.
⚠ Manual range selection must stay within a single verse. Press Escape or tap a word from a different verse to cancel range mode.
